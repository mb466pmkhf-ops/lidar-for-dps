## 2. Readings are estimates

FrameScout is a planning tool. Light readings, T-stops, exposure and colour figures, lux, beam sizes, the sun's path and shadows, room sizes and distances from a scan or a listing, camera framing and lens coverage, kit lists, power and amp totals, and the 3D previews are worked out from models, published figures and your phone's sensors. The app marks these figures as estimates. They will differ from what you get on set.

- Check exposure with a light meter and the camera before you shoot.
- Measure anything that has to fit (a dolly track, a crane, a doorway) with a tape.
- Check the sun on the day. Weather, buildings and trees aren't in the model.
- The camera and lens library comes from makers' published figures and our own working where those aren't published. Check the exact format and coverage with the rental house.
