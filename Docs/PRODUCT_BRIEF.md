# FrameScan — product brief

**Who:** directors of photography, camera operators, directors and location managers on a recce.

**Job:** understand a space fast and leave with a record the crew can trust: capture a room or location,
review its shape and dimensions, mark camera positions and subject marks, save it and share it.

## Principles

1. **Fast in the field.** Few taps from launch to scanning; large controls usable one-handed, in sunlight, with gloves.
2. **Honest numbers.** Every figure says whether it was *measured* (LiDAR/AR), *estimated* (heuristic or
   imprecise sensor) or *manual* (typed or corrected). Estimates are never dressed up as measurements.
3. **Works without LiDAR.** Unsupported devices or conditions get a plain explanation and a useful fallback
   (plane-based AR measure, viewfinder, notes, sun tools, manual dimensions), never a dead end.
4. **Private by default.** Scans, photos and GPS stay on the device until the user exports them.
5. **Recoverable capture.** Tracking loss, low light, featureless walls and thermal limits come with
   specific guidance on what to do next.
6. **Accessible.** VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type, meaning not carried by colour alone.

## Reliable first-use flow (current priority)

Check capability and permissions → guided scan → review and correct figures → save to a project → reopen it.

## Out of scope for now

Accounts, cloud sync, multi-user projects (local-first stays the default; see `Docs/ROADMAP.md`).
