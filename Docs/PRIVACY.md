# FrameScout privacy policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026. Draft: the owner fills in the bracketed parts before publishing._

FrameScout is a location-scouting and lighting-planning app for film crews, on iPhone, the web and
desktop. This policy says what it stores, where, and how to delete it.

## Without an account

Everything stays on your device: projects, LiDAR scans, meshes, photos, measurements, lighting plans
and the location's GPS position (used to work out the sun's path). Nothing is sent anywhere. There is
no tracking, advertising or analytics in any version of the app.

## With an account (sync)

If you sign in to sync between your devices, we store:

- **Your account:** email address, and which sign-in you used (Apple, Google or email and password).
  Passwords are stored hashed by the sync server; we never see them.
- **Your projects:** the project file and each scan's 2D plan: names, notes, the location address and
  GPS position, lights, people, cameras, measurements and the floor plan.

We do **not** upload scan meshes, textures or photos. They stay on the device that made them.

This data is used only to show your projects on your own devices. It is not sold, shared, or used
for advertising or profiling.

## Where it is stored

Sync runs on Supabase (supabase.com), in its London region (eu-west-2). Supabase processes the data
on our behalf. Sign-in with Apple or Google is handled by those companies under their own policies.

## Deleting your data

- In the app: **Account & sync → Delete account**. This removes your account and every synced project
  from the server straight away. Projects on your devices stay there until you delete them.
- Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.
- Or email [contact email] and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion at [contact email]. If you are
in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

FrameScout is a professional tool and isn't aimed at children under 13.
